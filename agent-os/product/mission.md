# Product Mission

## Pitch
Golf League Manager is a web application that helps golf league members and administrators manage their league activities by tracking tournament events, scores, and weekly league play in a centralized, easy-to-use platform.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- **League Members (Players):** Golfers participating in the league who need to track their performance and view schedules.
- **League Administrators:** Organizers responsible for managing the league's schedule, events, and results.

### User Personas
**The League Organizer** (35-65)
- **Role:** League Administrator
- **Context:** Volunteers or manages a local golf league, often using spreadsheets or paper.
- **Pain Points:** Manual score calculation errors, difficult to communicate schedule changes, time-consuming to compile results.
- **Goals:** Streamline event creation, automate score tracking, and easily communicate with members.

**The Avid Golfer** (25-55)
- **Role:** League Member
- **Context:** Plays weekly, wants to see how they rank and what upcoming events are scheduled.
- **Pain Points:** Unsure of tee times, hard to find past scores, misses announcements.
- **Goals:** Easily enter scores, view the leaderboard, and stay updated on league news.

## The Problem

### Manual League Management
Managing a golf league often involves scattered emails, spreadsheets, and manual scorecards. This leads to errors in scoring, confusion about schedules, and a lack of real-time engagement for players.

**Our Solution:** A dedicated web application built on the modern TALL stack (Laravel 12, Filament v3, Livewire v3, and Volt) that centralizes scheduling, scoring, and communication.

## Differentiators

### Integrated League Management
Unlike generic score tracking apps or manual spreadsheets, we provide a league-focused solution that handles the specific needs of weekly events and tournaments, including winner declaration and news broadcasting, all within a unified dashboard.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Score Entry:** Players can easily enter their scores for specific events.
- **Event Tracking:** View upcoming weekly league events and major tournaments.
- **News Feed:** A dedicated section for administrators to post announcements and updates.

### Collaboration Features
- **Leaderboards:** Automatically updated rankings based on entered scores.
- **Event Schedules:** shared calendar/list of upcoming tee times and locations.

### Advanced Features
- **Tournament Management:** Administrators can create special tournament events and officially declare winners.
- **Role-Based Access:** Distinct capabilities for Administrators (manage everything) vs. Players (view and enter own data).
